# Example prompts for MCP clients

## Draft a post

> Use `draft-post` / tools to create a draft titled "Q4 product update" summarizing our three new features. Keep status draft. Suggest categories.

## Moderate comments

> List pending comments. For each spam-like comment, trash with confirm. Approve genuine questions and draft a short reply.

## SEO audit

> For post ID 42, get current SEO title/description. Propose improved meta under 60/160 chars and update if Yoast or Rank Math is available.

## Read-only site brief

> Call site info and environment. List the five most recent posts. Do not modify anything.
